# Archivum identity integration — September 11, 2026

This supplement applies the owner's supplied `Archivum_Logo_Identity_Production_Pack.zip` and identity board to the implemented website. It supersedes older palette, wordmark, favicon, and decorative brand treatments in the frontend spec and concept images. The site structure, evidence semantics, and usability requirements remain in force.

The supplied visual board is included as [identity-board-2026-09-11.png](identity-board-2026-09-11.png).

## Brand colors

| Role | Exact identity color | Web use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graphite | `#0C0D0F` | Default page/stage ground; primary text on mineral |
| Mineral | `#F3F3F0` | Light page ground; primary text on graphite; archival materials |
| Silver | `#A8ADB4` | Secondary text on dark surfaces and spatial reference lines |
| Signal Blue | `#4DA3FF` | Selected objects, actions, focus, optional mark crossbar |

Functional surfaces use neutral intermediate grays. Small blue text on mineral uses the darker related `#1455B3` for legibility. Light-theme primary actions use `#174989` with white text; dark-theme actions use Signal Blue with Graphite text. Raw Signal Blue and Silver are not used as small text on mineral. Main text and action pairs are checked separately from decorative lines.

## Asset fidelity

- Navigation and footer use the official outlined wordmark. They do not recreate the logo using a font, add an invented symbol, or append a dot.
- `src/brand/` retains the full supplied packs (not web-served). Website-ready wordmarks live in `src/components/brand/wordmark-*.svg` and are bundled with the UI; only the viewBox/outer size is tightened around the visible artwork to remove the original export's uneven blank canvas. LinkedIn avatar and banner stay in `src/brand/` as social deliverables and are not used on website pages.
- `BrandWordmark` switches between the supplied monochrome versions for light and dark surfaces. It preserves aspect ratio.
- `BrandMark` reproduces the supplied compact A's polygon coordinates in an accessible decorative component. Optional Signal Blue appears only on the supplied upper crossbar.
- Browser/app icons use the supplied favicon, touch icon, and compact mark exports. Legal and domain lockups remain available for their appropriate uses.
- The provided looping motion SVG is retained as a reference asset, not embedded as a constant interface animation.

## Materials and details

Atlas remains the primary kinetic object: silver coordinate lines and quiet mineral points, with blue reserved for selection and relationships. The four passport layers separate and settle into readable HTML, echoing the identity's layers/separate/reassemble idea. Delisted plates are neutral mineral and silver on graphite; they have no decay effects, damage, flashing, or automatic orbit.

Geist remains the reading typeface. The wordmark itself uses supplied vector lettering, so it needs no font installation. Hierarchy comes from scale, spacing, and weight. The footer carries the identity line, “Evidence for a more intelligent tomorrow.” Compact A marks are used sparingly in passport and inspector contexts.
